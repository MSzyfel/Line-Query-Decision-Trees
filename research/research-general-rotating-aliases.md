# Rotating activation aliases tie every useful root-minority threshold

Auxiliary research note, 19 October 2026. The article is unchanged. This note
records a scalable greedy-bad seed, the precise limitation it establishes,
bounded completed-lift checks, and the team's subsequent information-filter
repair. It is **not** a fixed-point counterexample for the strongest generator.

## 1. A deterministic growing family

Let M=2^b, let k>=2 be fixed, and put R=M/2. Take

    H=F_M^k times [R]^ell,  ell=floor(M/log2 M).

The first k coordinates are selectors. Supply every pure coordinate equality,
with body equalities preceding gates in every tie. There are ell body
coordinates, all of the same alphabet R, so their equality minority is 2/M.
For each body j choose a nonzero slope a_j, with distinct slopes across
bodies, and the linear form

    f_j(c)=sum_{i=0}^{k-1} a_j^i c_i over F_M.

Partition selector contexts into the M/2 cells

    A(j,g)={c: f_j(c) lies in {2g,2g+1}}.

Here field elements are labeled by b-bit words, and the pair is one coset of
the one-dimensional binary subspace generated by1. Every cell has size
`2M^{k-1}`. Supply a complete b-1-bit body palette active precisely on that
cell. At an active context c it reads a bijective row label
`pi(j,g,c):[R]->F_2^{b-1}`. The maps can differ across c; require only that
each sends row zero to the all-one label. The checker uses context-dependent
coordinate rotations followed by complement, which are bijective and have
different meanings when b>=3.

The response matrix is explicit and polynomial in its n listed hypotheses.
The number of tests is

    kM+ell R+ell(M/2)(b-1)=O(M^2)

for fixed k and the displayed ell. Finite-field arithmetic is polynomial;
the bounded checker uses fixed primitive polynomials for M=4,8,16.

## 2. Full greedy gap survives the large activation sets

Every physical gate has root positive mass exactly1/M: its activation cell
has probability2/M and its bijective body bit is balanced. A pure selector
equality also has minority1/M. Pure body equalities have minority2/M.

**Lemma.** Ordinary balance greedy identifies every body by pure equalities
before asking a selector query or a gate. Its body cost is exactly

    ell e(R),  e(R)=(R+1)/2-1/R.

Selectors remain independent uniform until then. A gate on an already fixed
body has positive mass at most its activation probability2/M, so a fresh
body equality beats or ties it and wins the tie. For a partially searched
body with s remaining values, a palette bit initially had R/2 positive row
values. Its conditional gate positive probability is at most
`(2/M)(R/(2s))=R/(Ms)<1/s`, while its pure equality has minority1/s.
Gates on other unfixed bodies have mass1/M. Once a pure body equality is
chosen, greedy therefore finishes that body and then proceeds to the next.

A comparison first identifies all selectors by equality and then executes
each body's active palette. Its mean is at most

    k e(M)+ell(b-1)=O_k(M).

Greedy already pays `ell e(R)=Theta(M^2/log M)` on the bodies alone. Also
`log2 n=k log2 M+ell log2 R=Theta(M)`, so its ratio is
`Omega(log n/loglog n)`. Unlike the earlier near-M body alphabets, choosing
R=M/2 prevents fixed-body activation mass2/M from overtaking a fresh body
equality; the input priority handles that exact tie.

## 3. Minority filtering cannot isolate selector queries

There are exactly two distinct global root minorities:

    n/M: selector equalities and every gate;
    2n/M: body equalities.

A lower cutoff at most n/M retains all tests and adds no filtering. A larger
cutoff retains only body equalities. In particular no such threshold gives
the pure selector code used by the earlier filtered-source theorem.

More strongly, every body-only filtered conditional source, globally replayed
and completed with the cheaper of fresh greedy and actual incumbent
restriction, has exactly the initial incumbent's raw cost. Before every body
is known, its selector posterior remains uniform and its row posterior is a
product of surviving subsets. A remaining body equality has minority at
least2/M; every fixed-body gate has mass at most2/M; and the preceding
partial-row argument handles its own gates. Both fallbacks therefore finish
all remaining bodies using only equalities. On a uniform product, the mean
cost of a complete equality-only body decoder is always `ell e(R)`, regardless
of the adaptive coordinate/value order. Given the resulting full body tuple,
both fallbacks run exactly the same original-priority selector greedy code
as the incumbent. The context-source case is simpler: all body equalities
are constant, so its filtered source has no queries and its global replay
returns to the original incumbent.

This establishes a limitation of the **extra standalone threshold menu**.
It does not prove that every ordinary conditional source lift fails, nor
that retaining intermediate states of those extra codes cannot help an
expanded state-weaving generator.

On the actual all-body-zero incumbent slice, every gated bit is its entire
activation-cell indicator. Its minority2/M strictly beats pure selectors'
1/M, so its fresh greedy source is not a pure selector decoder. Its first
physical test also varies with body values globally. Thus the pure-projection
premise of completed selector replay is genuinely absent.

## 4. Actual bounded completed-lift checks

The checker directly builds the all-body-zero fresh greedy source, executes
its global replay on the real response table, and fills every actual exit
using the minimum of fresh greedy and pruned incumbent. This already includes
the fallback whose omission invalidates simpler activation counterexamples.

| M,k,ell | n | m | Initial raw cost | Body-zero completed lift |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4,2,2 | 64 | 16 | 400 | 422 |
| 4,3,2 | 256 | 20 | 2192 | 2288 |
| 8,1,2 | 128 | 32 | 1073 | 1097 |
| 8,1,3 | 512 | 44 | 5363 | 5431 |
| 8,2,1 | 256 | 28 | 2744 | 2792 |
| 8,2,2 | 1024 | 40 | 12992 | 13477 |

All six explicit source lifts are more expensive than the incumbent. These
are bounded facts, not asymptotic lower bounds. The k=1 cases are mechanism
checks outside the growing k>=2 class and are already repaired by the root
completion certificate for their supplied selector tests.

All 2,234 actual incumbent-node sources at the high body-only cutoff were
also executed, and every candidate cost equaled its incumbent exactly.
On the two smallest examples, the **entire current standalone filtered menu**
was run: 126 candidates at n=64 returned400, and510 at n=256 improved2192 to
2176. The latter improvement explicitly prevents treating this family as a
universal fixed point even for that weaker menu. The full lifting/weighted/
boundary/quadratic generator was not run here, and no exact OPT oracle or
random search was used.

Finite failures of one slice lift cannot be extrapolated. For example a
dirty branch's pure selector exclusion can raise a known-body activation
probability from2/M to2/(M-1), enough to beat fresh body equality2/M and let
fresh fallback learn an activation quotient before decoding more bodies.
This effect is especially direct for k=1 and could repair larger instances.

## 5. Root's information-filter repair

Root proposed a stronger, still polynomial response-family selection rule:
factor the high-cutoff body-only response support, take its whole body vector
Z, then retain original queries whose binary response is independent of Z
under the uniform prior. Independence is tested by exact contingency counts.

For this family the classification is exact:

* Every pure selector query is independent of the body vector.
* Every body equality depends on that vector.
* Every gate depends on its target body. Its unconditional positive
  probability is1/M, whereas conditional on that body's zero row it is2/M,
  because all local zero labels were chosen all-one.

Thus the retained family is precisely the pure selector equalities. Its
actual partial greedy code on H has full selector fibers as terminals; fresh
completion then gives the legal comparison `k e(M)+ell(b-1)`. No unavailable
activation test or outsider purity is assumed. For this class, the root's
information filter repairs both the missing support gap and the impure
incumbent slice.

The checker independently verifies exact joint-body independence for every
supplied test in all six examples, executes the resulting selector code,
checks its terminal fibers, and completes it with actual fresh/pruned
fallback. At M=8,k=1,ell=2 and3, the completed information-filter codes cost
1072 and5312, improving the incumbents1073 and5363. On the small other cases
the explicit comparison can exceed greedy; its asymptotic guarantee is the
comparison above, not a finite optimality claim.

Selecting tests individually independent of Z does **not** imply their
joint response vector is independent of Z in an arbitrary instance: XOR
synergy is a counterexample to that inference. The proposed general generator
can use independence as a selection heuristic and factor the resulting
actual support. The exact classification here is a proved property of this
family; no universal independence or approximation theorem follows.

## 6. Remaining adversarial direction

To survive this particular repair, aliases could balance each query across
all body values while preserving joint selector/body coupling. The all-one
zero-row construction would then be unavailable. That change also reduces
the conspicuous activation support in every fixed-body slice, potentially
restoring pure selector queries as greedy ties. It is not enough to posit
pairwise XOR camouflage: one must check all conditional sources, actual
completed replay costs, and the retained-family response factorization.

No deterministic scalable family surviving the strengthened information
filter plus completed lifting and state weaving has been proved here. The
saved results separate the proved greedy gap and threshold limitation from
the bounded lift failures and from the constructive information-filter escape.
